# Guardian — static review

## Scope and product boundary

Revision `73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570`; implementation/prototype, not a certified wallet. Runtime **NOT RUN**. The sampled CLI offers a [run cycle](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/index.ts#L168-L181): “snapshot → risk → plan → approve → execute”. The [run handoff](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/commands/run.ts#L171-L225) checks policy, obtains a decision and passes the plan and decision to execution. A separate [dashboard manifest](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/dashboard/package.json#L1-L18) identifies a Next.js app; landing and pitch are separate tree surfaces, not reviewed apps. Solana RPC, a signer and a model provider are external boundaries, not independently verified here.

## Findings ledger

- **GDN-1 — Medium, confirmed static:** The README's [pipeline](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/README.md#L19-L40) shows a “Human Approval Gate” before execution. The [routing code](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/approvals/approval.engine.ts#L31-L71) instead returns `"auto_approved"` for `policyOnly` + `ALLOWED`, and `"yolo"` for `never` on configured devnet; `never` off devnet escalates to human. Its warning is assembled from two literals: `"APPROVAL_MODE=never is only allowed on devnet. "` and `"Escalating to needs_human for safety."` Denied actions and no-action plans take earlier branches. [Configuration](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/config/loadConfig.ts#L21-L30) and [environment loading](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/config/loadConfig.ts#L72-L82) default approval mode to `always`; an explicit environment value overrides it. An operator could mistake the diagram for a universal human gate. Document the modes and ask the owner which are permitted.

- **GDN-2 — High, confirmed static boundary; downstream impact unverified:** [Execution](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/execute/execute.ts#L25-L57) checks `if (!approvalDecision.approved)` and invokes preflight outside simulation; it does not compare that decision's request or approved intent to the supplied plan. The [decision type](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/approvals/approval.types.ts#L25-L64) includes a request ID but no transaction-intent binding. The [router](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/execute/execute.ts#L68-L96) passes `plan.swapParams` or `plan.transferParams` downstream. A valid boolean alone does not establish that this plan is the one approved. Swap, transfer and signer internals were not inspected: no changed transaction, exploit or actual loss is established. Trace those paths, then bind and validate approved intent at the real signing boundary.

- **GDN-3 — Medium, confirmed static:** [Approval history loading](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/approvals/approval.store.ts#L16-L34) warns `"approvals.json is malformed. Starting fresh."` and returns an empty list on invalid JSON or non-array data. [Append/save](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/approvals/approval.store.ts#L39-L60) then writes the resulting array back. If malformed history is followed by an append, prior audit bytes can be overwritten; no actual loss was observed. Fail closed and preserve/quarantine the original, with tested recovery.

- **GDN-4 — Medium, confirmed static and historical CI:** [Root scripts](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/package.json#L9-L14) have build, dev, guardian and typecheck, but no test script. [CI](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L18-L23) says it avoids “failing on lock drift”, runs `npm install`, then `npm run build` only. [Actions run 36902672713](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/actions/runs/36902672713) is recorded successful at the pinned SHA; it is historical, not independently rerun or a safety test. Restore lockfile reproducibility and add behavioral gates without lowering checks.

- **GDN-5 — Medium, confirmed static sequence; recovery unverified:** After a successful execution, [run.ts](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/commands/run.ts#L231-L313) saves a pending receipt with `actionTxSignature`, processes it, and reports `"Execution succeeded but receipt failed."` on processing failure. [Receipt anchoring](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/receipts/receipt.anchor.ts#L9-L27) builds `guardian_receipt:v1:${receiptHash}` and calls `sendMemoTx`. This is after execution, so failure cannot undo an action. Whether pending-save failure, ambiguous status or retry handling prevents duplicate actions remains unverified; require reconciliation and an isolated recovery rehearsal.

## Five-discipline assessment

- **Correctness:** [Swap policy](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/policy/policy.engine.ts#L98-L153) checks allowed actions, mints, amount, slippage and projected spend, and collects approval reasons. [Preflight](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/execute/execute.guard.ts#L42-L78) checks `config.solanaNetwork === "mainnet-beta"`, a fee reserve, action type and policy revalidation. It does not prove RPC endpoint and actual chain match. Simulation skips this preflight in the inspected router.
- **Automation/testing:** GDN-4 is limited to sampled root scripts and workflow; it does not establish that no tests exist elsewhere. Behavioral and mutation gates are proposed, not existing controls.
- **Operations:** GDN-5 requires recovery without repeating the action. Daemon stop, backup and restore behavior, RPC outages, signer custody and release rollback are untested.
- **Security / OWASP:** Approval is not proof of intent binding (GDN-2). Authorization of policy edits, model-input injection, secret redaction outside the sampled [config summary](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/config/loadConfig.ts#L140-L156), data retention, replay protection and dependency integrity are unverified. The README's key-handling claims are not evidence of end-to-end protection. The receipt memo alone does not prove authorization or integrity of the entire workflow.
- **UI/UX/accessibility:** The [CLI prompt](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/approvals/approval.cli.ts#L34-L40) treats timeout as rejection and offers [y/n/d/a](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Guardian/blob/73fe9f7b28ffcf9c2aec1468bdd5b748dee18570/src/approvals/approval.cli.ts#L55-L88). Actual display completeness, accessibility and dashboard approval flow are unverified. A dashboard approval view is not assumed to exist.

## Production blockers and owner decisions

Before any release beyond isolated testing, the owner must choose allowed networks and approval modes, verify signer custody and policy-edit authority, decide what is irreversible, approve recovery/stop procedures, and establish whether a dashboard approval surface is wanted. Intent binding, persistence recovery, reproducible gates and independently observed stop/restore remain open. No production readiness or actual harm is claimed.
